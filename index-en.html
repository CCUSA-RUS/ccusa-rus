Choose your adventure

CCUSA has been running international exchange programs for nearly 40 years and sends students from around the world to the U.S. for work in camps and at independent employers in the tourism and recreation sectors.
In Russia, CCUSA offers two trusted programs: Camp Counselors USA and Work Experience USA. Choose the format that fits you best.

CAMP COUNSELORS USA

Work in American summer camps as a counselor or support staff member. Have the best summer of your life! Work, travel, and make friends from around the world. Accommodation and meals are provided by the camp.

WORK EXPERIENCE USA

Seasonal summer jobs in hotels, resorts, parks, and restaurants. Improve your English, earn money, and travel for up to 30 days after work.
